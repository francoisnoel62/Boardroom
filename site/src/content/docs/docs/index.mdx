BOARDROOM is a local decision workspace, built in the open on GitHub. You bring a question and its context; a team of AI advisers examines the evidence, challenges the proposal and works with you toward a plan you can stand behind. Disagreement can remain at the end, and **the final decision is always yours**.

Its source is public; the project license is still awaiting a decision, so it is not yet open source in the legal sense.

<Claim id="open-source-license" />

## Who it is for

Technical founders and builders who make consequential product and engineering decisions — often alone, and often before there is anything to measure.

## What you can run today

The current build completes **Plan 01**: a recorded example you can install and explore on macOS (Apple silicon), Windows x64 and Linux x64, without an account or a model key.

- Read a scripted, fictional discussion in which a sourced objection changes the plan.
- Inspect the exact saved revision behind each citation, including text, PDF and DOCX sources.
- Export a new plan and decision memo, then reopen your progress and export history.

<Aside type="note" title="Recorded, not live">
  The example is a scripted fixture with fictional model labels. Playback makes no model calls. Live meetings with your own models arrive with Plan 02.
</Aside>
