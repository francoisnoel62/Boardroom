The live lifecycle is implemented in the CLI and terminal, with deterministic installed tests. Plan 02 still awaits its real-account qualification campaign. The recorded example illustrates the same shape without calling a model.

## The phases

1. **Framing.** A question and its context are enough; an existing plan is optional. The Product Owner proposes a framing, and work waits for your approval of that exact version.
2. **Independent analysis.** Advisers receive the same factual baseline without seeing each other's first conclusions.
3. **Confrontation.** Objections target a specific claim or part of the proposal, say why it matters, and suggest a correction or a way to resolve the uncertainty.
4. **Revision.** The Product Owner publishes a new proposal version. Earlier views stay attached to the version they judged.
5. **Final views.** Each adviser states a verdict on the same immutable version. You then accept, reject, modify, defer or ask for more investigation.

## In the recorded example

Proposal v1 plans five integrations in four weeks. The Lead Developer cites `context.md` line 4 — two engineers — and the Marketing Manager cites line 6. Proposal v2 narrows to one integration and five design partners. Two advisers approve; the Marketing Manager keeps `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`. The human decision stays `pending`.
