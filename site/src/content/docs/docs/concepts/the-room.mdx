A BOARDROOM meeting brings advisers with distinct responsibilities to one question. **You are the CEO**: you frame the question, add facts, challenge arguments and make the decision. Advisers inform it; they never make it.

## Today's recorded example

The recorded example seats three advisers, each with a fictional model label:

| Seat | The question it brings |
| :--- | :--- |
| Product Owner | What is the smallest thing we can build that solves a problem worth solving? |
| Lead Developer | What will it take to make this real, and which constraint could break the plan? |
| Marketing Manager | Who needs this, how do we reach them, and what evidence says they will pay? |

The Product Owner authors the shared proposal; the others challenge and amend it. There is no moderator adviser.

## The room you will configure

The product direction is a team whose size, roles and models you choose for each decision — a logistics expert for a supply question, a finance director for an investment. No delivery plan schedules this yet.
