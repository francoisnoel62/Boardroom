Live meetings are not available in this build. This page states what Plan 02 commits to, so you know what to expect; it will become a step-by-step guide once that milestone is accepted.

## What Plan 02 commits to

- A real question answered by **three distinct models from at least two providers**, with your own API keys.
- Independent first analyses, then challenges against specific claims, a revised proposal and final views on that same version.
- A duration target and a cost ceiling, with partial results labelled as partial.
- Your decision recorded separately from the advisers' views.

Selected context will then be sent to the providers you configure, under their pricing and terms. Nothing is sent in the current build.
